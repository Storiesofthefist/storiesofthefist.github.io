Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how "Stories of the Fist Poster" (the "App") handles data. The App is a personal tool the Stories of the Fist creator uses to post their own videos to their own TikTok account.

1. Data the App uses

  • TikTok account access: when the creator signs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name and open ID). These are used only to post videos to that account and to check post status.
  • Videos and captions: the clips and captions the creator chooses to publish.

2. Data the App does not collect

The App does not collect data from viewers, followers or any other TikTok users. It does not track anyone, show ads or sell data.

3. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored securely by the posting service the App uses (Composio) and are used only to post on the creator's behalf. Videos are uploaded temporarily so they can be sent to TikTok.

4. Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok, to publish videos, and with the service providers that make posting possible. It is never sold.

5. Removing access

The creator can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
